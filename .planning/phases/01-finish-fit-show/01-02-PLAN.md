<objective>
Change the fit & show score update path from optimistic-locking (reject on concurrent
edit) to last-write-wins (D-02), and add the admin-only bulk finalize capability the
frontend needs for a global "Finalize Scoring" action (D-12) plus a reusable, reason-
aware per-score finalize for D-11's admin-override audit trail.

Purpose: The current `updateFitShowScore` rejects a second judge's concurrent save with a
`ConditionalCheckFailedException` (surfaced client-side as a conflict) — this is the
opposite of the user's locked D-02 decision and must be relaxed. Separately, there is
today no way to lock every score at once; only a single-score `finalizeFitShowScore`
mutation exists.

Output: `updateFitShowScore` silently accepts the most recent write regardless of a stale
in-flight version; a new `finalizeAllFitShowScores` mutation, resolver case, CDK resolver
mapping, and data-access method exist and are admin-gated.
</objective>
